Task pwn01_01: Intro Challenge
The government recently hired the "IT-Security consultant" Kasper McNorton to digitalize their secret documents.

After a few months of work he presents his first solution: a simple server that stores the secret behind a password. He urged the government to keep his code secret, as to not compromise the security. However, when the responsible agency faxed the code to the TüV for certification, the Übungsleitung tapped the line and got a copy of the code. You can find it in this zipfile from the scoreboard.

Exercise 1 is hosted at netsec.net.in.tum.de at port 20001. This is an intro practice challenge, no points are awarded for solving it. We still encourage you to solve it to get a feel for how the challenges work.

General Tips
For plain text challenges, we recommend connecting to the server using netcat (man nc) first.
It is advisable to have a Linux system at hand for the challenges.
Our servers and clients are written in Python.
